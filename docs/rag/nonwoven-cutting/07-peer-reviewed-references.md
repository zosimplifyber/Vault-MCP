# Peer-Reviewed References on Ultrasonic Cutting and Bonding of Nonwovens

Four papers, with a coverage-gap note. All four study **thermoplastic** nonwovens or
thermoplastic monofilament.

## The coverage gap

**All four papers study thermoplastic nonwovens or thermoplastic monofilament.** Nylon
monofilament in the Coatings paper; polypropylene medical and hygiene nonwoven in the
other three.

There is **no published parameter study for ultrasonic or knife cutting of
non-thermoplastic cellulosic or foam-formed webs.** The process window for that material
class has to be established experimentally. This is not a search failure; the literature
does not exist because the industrial demand driving the research has been hygiene and
medical PP.

## How to use these papers given the gap

The papers remain useful in three specific ways, none of which is direct parameter
transfer.

1. **As mechanism, not as settings.** The INJ bonding paper explains what forms the seal.
   Understanding that mechanism is what tells you it will not occur on cellulose, which is
   the central finding of this collection.
2. **As experimental method.** The IJCST nested ANOVA design is a ready-made structure for
   a trial matrix over the four interacting ultrasonic parameters: amplitude, applied
   pressure, cutting speed and blade angle.
3. **As a measurement vocabulary.** The Coatings paper's edge-quality characterisation
   gives a defensible way to score cut edges on a novel material, even though its
   parameter values do not carry over.

## Where to search instead

For a cellulosic or foam-formed sheet, the **paper and board converting literature** is a
closer physical analogue than PP nonwoven literature. Die cutting and rotary cutting of
paperboard and moulded fibre deal with the same problem: a cellulosic, non-melting,
fibrous, often lofty web that has to be cut cleanly without a thermal seal.

Related search direction: **edge stabilisation by binder application** rather than by
thermal fusion, since thermal fusion is unavailable on this material class.
