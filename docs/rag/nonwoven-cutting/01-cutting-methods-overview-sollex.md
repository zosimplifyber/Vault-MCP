# Industrial Cutting Methods for Nonwoven Fabrics — Sollex Overview

Source: Sollex, industrial blade manufacturer, Malmö, Sweden.
https://www.sollex.com/blog/post/industrial-cutting-methods-for-nonwoven-fabrics

Sollex manufactures and resharpens the blades themselves, so this source is strongest on
blade geometry, coatings and part numbers, and weakest on machine-level process
parameters.

## Rotary cutting of nonwoven fabric

Uses a rotating circular knife. Suited to thick nonwovens or several layers at once, and
to high-speed continuous production. Sollex stresses that the circular knife needs "extra
long and sharp grinding" for precision, and offers both manufacture and resharpening for
nonwoven-specific circular knives.

## Razor slitting of nonwoven fabric

The material is tensioned on a rewinder machine and cut by fixed industrial razor blades.
Suited to thin nonwovens on spools and to high-speed operation.

**Web tension is the dominant process variable.** Blade edge coatings reduce heat build-up
and extend blade life.

Sollex reports that "blades 5K, 5Z as well as 2-013-Z showed excellent results" on this
class of work.

Note that the stated intended materials are films rather than nonwovens; the recommendation
for nonwoven slitting is Sollex's own, based on results rather than on the catalogue
description.

## Plotter and CNC oscillating knife cutting of nonwoven fabric

An oscillating knife driven by CNC over material held flat on a vacuum table. Sollex
describes it as giving a "high degree of precision and accuracy" and as the method for
complex designs and patterns.

Representative oscillating blade geometry for this work: 45° pointed blade,
50 x 6 x 1.5 mm, 20 mm cutting depth (Sollex Z46). Blades reaching 30 mm cutting depth
are available.

## Die cutting of nonwoven fabric

A shaped metal die is pressed vertically through the material against a cutting press.
A straight serrated or toothed knife performs the equivalent action for straight cuts.
Sollex makes custom toothed knives to order, and notes that not all of them are listed
online.

## Ultrasonic cutting of nonwoven fabric

Sollex characterises ultrasonic cutting as "hundreds of times faster" than an oscillating
knife, fast and precise, and the method of choice for complex shapes in medical and
hygiene products. See `02-ultrasonic-cutting-fundamentals-rinco.md` for the mechanism and
its thermoplastic dependency.

## Laser cutting of nonwoven fabric

A high-powered laser passed through the material. Sollex positions it for medical and
hygiene products such as surgical masks and gowns.

## Method selection by material thickness

Sollex's stated selection logic: "Thickness of the nonwoven material can affect the type
of cutting method used, as well as the cutting parameters, such as blade depth and speed.
Thicker nonwoven materials may require more force or a different type of cutting tool."
